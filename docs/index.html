An assessment of the order creation to Truckbase invoicing workflow, ahead of release. The money is right — an order’s rate is preserved exactly through invoicing, verified end to end. The recommendation is nonetheless to hold, on two critical defects: a negative rate produces a billable invoice, and a created invoice cannot leave the system.
