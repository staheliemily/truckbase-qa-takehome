## Ranking rationale

_Why the risks are ordered the way they are — likelihood, blast radius, how quickly
it would be noticed in production, and how hard it is to unwind after the fact._

## Scenarios prioritized, and why

_The scenarios chosen for automation, each tied back to the risk it covers and what
a failure would actually tell you._

## Edge cases considered

_Boundaries, odd inputs, and state transitions worth probing — and which of them
made it into a test versus stayed a note._

## Not tested, and why

_Deliberate exclusions: out of scope, low risk, better covered at another layer,
or not feasible in the time available._

## Assumptions

_What was taken as given about the app, the data, the environment, and the account —
each one a thing that would change the approach if wrong._
